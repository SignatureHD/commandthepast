Where it begins

Period photograph British soldiers on the slopes of Spion Kop, January 1900. (Imperial War Museum, public domain — placeholder for drop-in.)
23–24 Jan 1900 Natal Anglo-Boer War Relief of Ladysmith Boer Victory

Battle of Spion Kop

On 24 January 1900, British forces seized the summit of Spion Kop by night — only to find themselves trapped in shallow trenches, surrounded by Boer guns on three sides. In one of the most tragic misunderstandings of the war, Colonel Thorneycroft abandoned the hill at midnight, unaware the Boers were equally close to breaking.
