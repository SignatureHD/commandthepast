How to play
  1. Select a British unit (red) by clicking it — yellow border shows it is selected.
  2. Yellow highlighted hexes show where it can move. Click a hex to move there.
  3. Select your Royal Artillery unit and switch to Fire mode to bombard visible Boer positions.
  4. Click End Turn to let Botha respond — watch his commandos reposition and his guns open up.
  5. Hold 2 or more units on the Spion Kop summit for 6 consecutive turns to win. Don't let British strength fall below 12.
The game includes fog of war — you can only see what your units can see. Units on high ground see further. The Boer Long Tom on Conical Hill has range 4. Use your artillery early.

This scenario is designed to help users understand terrain, command pressure, and historical uncertainty. It does not minimise the human cost of the battle. Spion Kop cost roughly 243 British lives and an estimated 68 Boer lives on a single day.
