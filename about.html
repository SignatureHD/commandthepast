— About

About Command The Past

An educational history project — built for curious readers, students, battlefield tourists, and history lovers.

01 / Scope

What Command The Past is

Command The Past is an interactive educational history platform. Each entry combines historical narrative, terrain analysis, commander profiles, and a browser-based strategy scenario — built to help people understand not just what happened in a battle, but why it happened, and what might have been different.

The platform is not a war game. It does not glorify casualties or trivialise the human cost of conflict. It is designed to make military history accessible, meaningful, and genuinely understandable — through terrain, command pressure, and the uncertainty that every commander faces.

It begins with the Anglo-Boer War because that conflict shaped modern South Africa in ways that are still visible today — in place names, in families, in the landscape of the Battlefields Route that runs through KwaZulu-Natal. But the archive will expand.

02 / Approach

Our approach to history

Command The Past separates historical record from interactive interpretation.

Battle pages are built from cited historical sources. Where figures — casualties, troop strengths, timing — are uncertain or contested between sources, they are clearly labelled as estimates. A confidence framework (verified / probable / contested / unknown) is used throughout to distinguish what we know from what we believe.

— 01

Verified record

Facts confirmed by multiple cited sources. Shown without qualification.

— 02

Probable / estimated

Consistent across most sources but exact figures vary. Always labelled.

— 03

Interactive interpretation

Game scenarios and alternate decisions. Educational tools, not historical claims.

Interactive game scenarios use simplified decision models to help users experience terrain, timing, command pressure, and plausible alternatives. They are not historical simulations. They are educational tools. The difference matters.

We do not invent certainty. We do not copy copyrighted source material. We do not present one side's narrative as the complete truth. The Anglo-Boer War was fought by people on both sides who believed in what they were doing — and who suffered for it. Both perspectives deserve honest treatment.

03 / Stack

How it was built

Command The Past was built using Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. The interactive game is a self-contained hex strategy scenario built with HTML5 Canvas — historically grounded in the actual terrain of Spion Kop, with fog of war, ranged artillery, morale routing, and an AI opponent modelled on Louis Botha's historical defence. It was built with Claude.

The site is designed to expand — each new battle follows the same data model and component architecture, making it straightforward to add new entries as research is completed and verified.
